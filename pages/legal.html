Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: June 25, 2026 · TradeBoard, LLC

1. Introduction

TradeBoard, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Cajun AI website and the TradeBoard platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (hashed — never stored in plain text).

Broker Connections

TradeBoard connects to brokerage accounts using OAuth 2.0 authorization only. We never store your broker username, password, or login credentials. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest in our database and are used solely to retrieve your trade data on your behalf.

Trade Data

We retrieve and store your trade history, positions, and portfolio data to power the TradeBoard analytics dashboard. This data is associated with your account and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Usage Data

We may collect anonymized usage data (page views, feature usage) to improve the platform. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the TradeBoard platform
  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • To retrieve and display your trade data from connected brokers
  • To send transactional emails (account alerts, notifications you configure)
  • To comply with applicable law

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest) to protect your data. OAuth tokens are encrypted in our database. We conduct regular security reviews and do not expose credentials to third parties.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services including payment processors and cloud hosting providers. These providers have their own privacy policies and are contractually required to handle your data securely.

7. Cookies

We use essential session cookies to maintain your login state. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing

9. Contact
