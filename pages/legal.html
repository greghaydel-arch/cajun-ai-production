Privacy Policy & Terms

Editorial review draft: October 16, 2026 · Not approved or effective

1. Introduction

Cajun AI LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Cajun AI website and the TradeBoard platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

The invited application flow uses provider-backed email authentication, invitation and acceptance records, and a server-side session. A local password-account description is inaccurate. Provider identity and configuration must be verified for the actual release.

Trade History Imports

Initial invited TradeBoard testing uses an approved trade-history CSV format. Direct broker OAuth or API connections are not enabled for this release. Do not include brokerage login credentials in uploaded files. Any future read-only broker integration will require separate vendor and security approval and updated disclosures.

Trade Data

TradeBoard may process trade-history records that invited users upload for retrospective analytics and journaling. The exact categories stored, data retention, and third-party processing arrangements require verification against the release implementation before publication.

Usage Data

The marketing website loads external fonts and embeds a Zoho waitlist. The TradeBoard marketing build includes Runable analytics. Actual request metadata, identifiers, cookies, lawful basis, retention and processor terms require review; anonymity has not been established.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the TradeBoard platform
  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • To process historical trade records that you choose to import
  • To send transactional emails (account alerts, notifications you configure)
  • To comply with applicable law

4. Data Security

We use safeguards intended to protect information in our services. Specific encryption, hosting, access-control, and retention descriptions must be checked against the audited production environment before finalizing this policy.

5. Data Retention

The application includes asynchronous account deletion, privacy exports and cleanup retries. Provider copies, backups and restore-suppression evidence have distinct retention needs. Immediate deletion of all copies is not established; approve category-specific periods and exceptions before publication.

6. Third-Party Services

The marketing pages use Zoho Forms and Google Fonts. Current application source selects Auth0 and Zoho transactional delivery and references DigitalOcean PostgreSQL and object storage. Verify actual enabled vendors, contracts, regions and retention. Billing is disabled in the first invited release; future payments and YouTube embeds require updated disclosures.

7. Cookies

The application uses a server-managed session cookie. Website preview flags use browser storage and do not authenticate users. External form/font requests and marketing analytics require a separate inventory before making any no-tracking or essential-only claim.

8. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing

9. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact us at: @cajunai1001 on X or via Instagram at @cajun_ai.

TradeBoard initially accepts historical trade records through a validated CSV format for invited testing. Direct broker connections and automatic sync are not enabled. Any future broker-data integration requires separate security, vendor, and disclosure review before being advertised or offered.
