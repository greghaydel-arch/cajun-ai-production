Perplexity AI Review — The Research Tool That's Actually Useful

We've been using Perplexity daily. Here's what it's good at, where it falls short vs Google, and whether the Pro plan is worth $20/mo.

By Cajun AI Editorial·May 2026·5 min read
Note: This review reflects Perplexity AI as of May 2026. Features and pricing are subject to change — verify at perplexity.ai before subscribing.

Most people hear "AI search" and think it's just ChatGPT with a search bar bolted on. Perplexity is something more specific than that — and understanding what it actually does well is the key to knowing whether it belongs in your workflow.

The short version: Perplexity is not a search engine replacement for everything. But for a specific class of research tasks — finding current information with source citations you can actually verify — it's the best tool available right now.

What Perplexity Actually Is

Perplexity is an AI-powered answer engine. You ask a question, it searches the web in real time, synthesizes information from multiple sources, gives you a direct answer, and shows you numbered citations inline so you know exactly where each claim came from.

That last part — the citations — is what differentiates it. When ChatGPT (without browsing enabled) answers a question, it draws on its training data and you have no way to verify whether what it's telling you is current or accurate. Perplexity shows its work. Every factual claim is linked to a source.

What It's Great At

  • Current events and fast-moving topics: Anything where "what's true right now" matters — stock news, product launches, regulatory changes, sports results, software updates. Google is fine for this, but Perplexity synthesizes multiple sources into a single answer instead of making you click through ten links.
  • Research with source requirements: If you're writing something where you need to cite your sources, Perplexity's inline citation format makes that dramatically faster than traditional search.
  • Comparing products/services: "What are the differences between X and Y" queries return synthesized comparison summaries instead of making you read five review articles and triangulate yourself.
  • Technical questions with recent answers: Stack Overflow-style questions where the right answer depends on the current version of a library or tool. LLMs trained on older data often give you outdated syntax.

Where It Falls Short

  • Deep document creation: Perplexity isn't a writing assistant. For anything longer than a few paragraphs, you want Claude or ChatGPT.
  • Navigational searches: If you're looking for a specific website, Google is faster. "Perplexity, go to my bank's login page" is the wrong tool for the job.
  • Complex reasoning chains: Multi-step logical problems, coding help, mathematical derivations — use a proper LLM, not Perplexity.
  • Source quality: Perplexity can cite unreliable sources. The inline citations help, but you still need to apply judgment about source credibility.
  • Privacy: Like all AI tools, what you search is processed by their systems. Avoid entering personally identifiable information or sensitive data.

Free vs. Pro

The free tier gives you 5 Pro searches per day — enough to evaluate whether you like it. Pro ($20/mo) removes limits, adds more powerful underlying models (GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 as options), file uploads, and API access.

Honest take: if you use it primarily for research and fact-checking, the free tier covers a lot of ground. Pro is worth it if Perplexity becomes a primary daily workflow tool or if you need to upload documents for analysis.

✓ What We Like

  • Real-time web access with inline source citations
  • Synthesizes multiple sources into clean answers
  • Fast — faster than ChatGPT browsing mode
  • Pro lets you choose underlying model
  • Spaces feature for saved, shareable research threads

✗ What We Don't

  • Can still cite low-quality sources
  • Not a substitute for long-form writing or coding help
  • Mobile app is good but not as capable as desktop
  • $20/mo is hard to justify for casual users

The Verdict

Perplexity is a genuinely useful addition to an AI toolkit — not a replacement for anything you're already using, but a better version of search for research-heavy workflows. If you regularly need to find current, citable information, it's faster and more useful than opening ten tabs.

Try the free tier for a week. If you're using all 5 daily Pro searches before noon, pay for it. If you're not, you probably don't need Pro.
