What Is Generative AI? A Plain-English Guide

How generative systems produce content, where retrieval fits, and why their answers need verification.

By Cajun AI Editorial · Updated October 22, 2026 · Educational guide

Editorial update: October 22, 2026. Educational content; not a product benchmark or professional advice.

Generative AI produces outputs such as text, images or audio from patterns learned during training and the context supplied at use time. A fluent answer is not evidence that the answer is correct.

Models, applications and tools

A language model generates sequences of tokens. A token can be a word, part of a word or punctuation; it is not always a whole word. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are applications or product families built around models and additional tools. Some modes can retrieve documents or search the web. That makes “AI never looks anything up” an inaccurate description of the whole application.

The 2017 paper Attention Is All You Need introduced the Transformer architecture. It is an important foundation for language models, not a complete description of every image, audio or video system.

A practical verification habit

Separate a draft from a verified result. Open cited sources, check dates and calculations, and test generated code before relying on it. OpenAI explains that ChatGPT can produce incorrect facts and fabricated citations even when it sounds confident.

Use material you are allowed to share, review the service’s data controls, and treat high-stakes decisions as requiring qualified human judgment. No universal video length, reasoning ability or productivity improvement follows simply from a product using AI.
