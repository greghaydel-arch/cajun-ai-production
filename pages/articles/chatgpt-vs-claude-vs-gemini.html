ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini: How to Compare Them

A repeatable comparison checklist without unsupported rankings, subscription prices or claims of hands-on testing.

By Cajun AI Editorial · Updated October 22, 2026 · Educational guide

Editorial update: October 22, 2026. Educational content; not a product benchmark or professional advice.

There is no documented Cajun AI benchmark behind the former rankings on this page. This guide instead describes a comparison you can reproduce with your own permitted, non-sensitive material.

Compare the task, not just the brand

  1. Choose a representative writing, coding or research task with an answer you can check.
  2. Record the date, product, model where shown, plan and enabled tools. Keep the input and evaluation criteria consistent.
  3. Assess factual accuracy, instruction following, source support and the amount of correction needed.
  4. Repeat on several examples before drawing a conclusion. One impressive answer is not a reliability measurement.

Check sources and access separately

Web access, connected applications and limits depend on the selected product mode and account. They should not be assumed from a brand name. Google’s source-checking guidance says that Gemini can still make mistakes when sources are shown. OpenAI’s accuracy guidance describes similar reasons to verify ChatGPT output.

Compare current official plan terms before paying. No price, quota, universal winner or business-data approval is established here. Keep the evaluation independent of TradeBoard: AI capabilities are outside its initial invited-release scope.
