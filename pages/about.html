About Us

Built in Louisiana. Built for real use.

We're a small Louisiana-based tech company that builds software people actually use — trading tools, AI content, and more.

Our Story

From IT veteran to software founder

Cajun AI started from a simple frustration: existing trading tools were either too basic, too expensive, or built by people who don't actually trade. So we built our own.

What began as a personal trading dashboard has grown into TradeBoard — a full analytics and automation platform designed for retail traders who take their craft seriously.

We're also passionate about AI education. Through our YouTube channel and articles, we break down the tools and trends shaping the tech industry — in plain language, without the hype.
